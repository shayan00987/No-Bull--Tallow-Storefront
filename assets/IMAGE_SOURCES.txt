Product photography is referenced from the original public store's Shopify CDN in script.js and index.html.
The storefront uses remote images so the package can be small. For an offline/self-hosted production launch,
download authorized original product image files into this folder and update the image paths in script.js.
Product imagery and trademarks belong to No Bull Tallow. Obtain the store owner's permission before publishing
a branded commercial storefront or re-hosting their product photography.
